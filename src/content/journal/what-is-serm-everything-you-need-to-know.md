<p>In today’s digital age, it’s more important than ever for businesses to have a solid online presence. With so many people turning to the internet to research products and services before purchasing, companies must ensure their online reputation is in good standing.&nbsp;</p>



<p><br>Search algorithms are designed to provide a user with a set of links that create an objective picture of the issue. Sending a request about a brand, a user will get a link to an official website, a knowledge panel answer, a company rating, and links to review platforms.</p>



<p><br>A business can not predict which resources will be displayed on SERP. However, a company can affect what information will be presented there on the most trustable independent websites.</p>



<p><strong>That’s where SERM comes in.</strong></p>



<p><br>SERM, or Search Engine Reputation Management, is the approach to managing a company&#8217;s online reputation in search engine results pages (SERPs). It involves monitoring and analysing search results for keywords related to a business and taking action to ensure favourable results.</p>



<p><br>The goal of SERM is to influence the perception of a business in the eyes of potential customers who search for information about the company online. This is accomplished through a variety of tactics, such as:</p>



<p><br><strong>01</strong> Search engine optimization (SEO): optimising a business’s website (metatags, images, page content, loading speed) and online content to appear higher in search results for relevant keywords.</p>



<p><br><strong>02</strong> Content marketing: creating and promoting content that portrays the business positively can help influence search results.</p>



<p><br><strong>03</strong> Online reviews and reputation management: monitoring and responding to reviews and actively seeking positive reviews can help improve a business’s online reputation.</p>



<p><br><strong>04</strong> Social media management: engaging with customers and sharing positive content on social media platforms can help to build a positive online reputation.</p>



<p><br>SERM is important for businesses because search engine results can significantly impact a company’s performance. SERP becomes the first touchpoint of a potential customer with the business.<br></p>



<ul>
<li>The first impression matters. If a user gets a negative impression, the customer journey may end. Negative reviews, unfavourable news articles, or even just outdated information can all appear in search results and create a negative perception of a business.</li>
</ul>



<p><br>By actively managing their online reputation through SERM, businesses can control what information is displayed to potential customers in SERP and ensure that it accurately represents their brand. This approach can help build customer trust, increase brand awareness, and ultimately drive sales.</p>



<p><br>To conclude, SERM is an essential part of digital marketing and PR strategy for businesses in today’s digital age.</p>



<p><br>By managing their online reputation through SEO, content marketing, reputation management, and social media management, businesses can ensure that they present a positive image to potential customers and build trust and credibility in the online marketplace.</p>
